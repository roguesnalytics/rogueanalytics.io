The Problem

You have data. You just can't do anything useful with it.

Most businesses collect plenty of information — in their software, their spreadsheets, their invoices. The problem isn't the data. It's that nobody's turned it into something you can actually use to make better decisions.

That's where we come in. We don't just hand you a report — we help you figure out what questions to ask, find the answers in your data, and show you what to do about it.

📊
Your reports don't help you decide anything

You get weekly numbers but they don't tell you what's wrong or what to do next.

🗂️
Your data lives in five different places

QuickBooks, your CRM, spreadsheets, text messages — nothing talks to anything else.

⏱️
You're spending hours on stuff that should take minutes

You or someone on your team manually pulls numbers every week. It eats up time that should go to running the business.

🤷
You're making big calls without the full picture

Hiring, pricing, which customers to go after — you're going on gut because the data just isn't clear.

What We Do

We make your data actually work for you.

We offer a range of services depending on what your business needs — from a one-time project to ongoing support as your part-time analytics partner.

📐

Reporting & Dashboards

We build clean, easy-to-read dashboards that show you what's happening in your business — updated automatically, no manual work required.

Good for
Owners who are tired of digging for numbers every week, or teams that need a single place to see how things are going.
🔍

Digging Into a Specific Problem

Something doesn't add up — a job went sideways, a product isn't selling, a market feels off. We dig into your data and come back with a clear answer and a recommendation.

Good for
Businesses with a specific question they need answered, or owners who want to understand what's driving a problem before spending money to fix it.
📈

Forecasting & Planning

We use your historical data to build honest projections for revenue, demand, or growth — so you can plan ahead instead of always reacting.

Good for
Businesses heading into a busy season, planning a hire, or trying to figure out how fast they can realistically grow.
💡

Pricing & Profitability

We look at your margins, your customers, and your market to help you figure out where you're leaving money on the table — and how to get it back.

Good for
Businesses that feel busy but not profitable, or owners who haven't revisited their pricing in a while and aren't sure if it still makes sense.
⚙️

Automating the Manual Stuff

We find the repetitive data tasks your team does every week and build systems to handle them automatically — freeing up your time for the work that actually matters.

Good for
Teams that spend hours every week pulling reports, updating spreadsheets, or manually moving data between systems.
🤝

Ongoing Analytics Support

We become your part-time analytics partner — available on a regular basis to answer questions, run analysis, and help you keep improving how you use data over time.

Good for
Businesses that aren't ready for a full-time analyst but want senior-level help on a consistent basis without the overhead.
